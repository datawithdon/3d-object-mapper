3D Object Mapper

Upload photos of an object from different angles → get a rotatable 3D point cloud.

Tips for a good scan
  • Use 10–30 photos, same camera, taken all the way around the object.
  • Keep ~60–80% overlap between consecutive shots (small steps, not big jumps).
  • Good, even lighting and a textured/detailed object surface help a lot.
  • Avoid plain, reflective, or transparent objects — feature matching needs visual texture.
